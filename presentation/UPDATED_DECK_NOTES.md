# Tshwane RapidLink — Updated Pitch Notes

This seven-slide deck describes the working RapidLink PWA and clearly separates the current MVP from future integrations.

## Slide 1 — Tshwane RapidLink

RapidLink connects three operational views: the person requesting help, the responder receiving the offer, and the supervisor managing responder eligibility.

## Slide 2 — Background

- The City of Tshwane had approximately 4.0 million residents in Census 2022.
- Emergency response spans different services and operational paths.
- RapidLink proposes a shared digital request and status workflow rather than replacing emergency personnel.

Source: [Statistics South Africa — Census 2022 municipal factsheet](https://census.statssa.gov.za/assets/documents/2022/Census_2022_Municipal_factsheet-Web.pdf)

## Slide 3 — Problem statement

Emergency reporting can involve several handoffs: explaining the emergency, establishing location, determining availability, assigning help, and communicating progress. Citizens often lack a shared status view after making the initial report.

## Slide 4 — Solution

The current prototype demonstrates:

1. SOS or service-specific activation.
2. Browser location capture and landmark details.
3. Nearest compatible station selection.
4. Offers sent to eligible, on-duty responders.
5. First-responder acceptance and visible progress through arrival and client-confirmed completion.

It is an installable PWA. The workflow can run locally offline on one device, while cross-device coordination synchronizes online.

## Slide 5 — Existing solutions

- Gauteng e-Panic is a free provincial panic-button initiative delivered through a mobile app and physical devices, with responses coordinated through a command centre.
- Namola offers consumer SOS, public coordination, and paid private-response tiers through an operator-led response centre.
- AURA is a B2B platform that embeds a private response network into partner products.
- Tshwane 107 is the City’s toll-free, 24-hour voice channel into its Emergency Services Communication Centre.

Sources:

## Slide 6 — Uniqueness

RapidLink’s differentiation is the complete municipal workflow demonstrated in one product:

- Direct, service-aware responder offers.
- A combined police and ambulance SOS path.
- Attendance and workload-based eligibility.
- Local offline continuity with queued synchronization.
- Client confirmation before an incident is closed.

## Slide 7 — Commercialisation

- Pilot: one station and one ward for 30 days.
- City deployment: annual municipal platform licence plus implementation and support.
- Multi-metro: configurable station, service, workflow, and governance deployment.
- Citizens do not pay a subscription.

Suggested pilot measures: time to first offer, acceptance time, progress-update completion, cancelled-alert handling, and client-confirmed closure.

## Accuracy boundary

The following are roadmap items and are intentionally not presented as completed MVP capabilities: SMS/USSD fallback, zero-rated access, AI triage, live responder-map tracking, hospital pre-alert, and production integration with municipal dispatch systems.
